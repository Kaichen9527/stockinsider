# T06 and T10 TWSE reduction and listing evidence

Two listed companies with mismatches between the November 15 company roster and custody report have published stock-replacement events. The issuer's September disclosure schedules 1808's old-share suspension for November 12–20 and new-share listing for November 23. TWSE's reduction report identifies 3714's prior trading date as November 4 and schedules its return for November 16. TAIFEX's separate notice refers to the same stock-replacement announcement and shows its futures suspension from November 5 to November 13. A temporary old-share stop and the replacement listing do not establish permanent issuer delisting.
