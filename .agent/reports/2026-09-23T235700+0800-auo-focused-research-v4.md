## Calculation policy

The three existing operating segments still drive 2026/2027 normalised EPS; CPO and GCS inputs are null until public capacity, utilization, yield, ASP, costs, intercompany eliminations and attributable ownership can be checked. The reverse-price table uses 5%/10%/15% **hypothetical after-tax attributable margins** as required-revenue thresholds, not expected orders. Existing 20x/24x are labeled uncalibrated sensitivity coordinates; 2029 year-end values now discount approximately 3.25 years from the research date. P/B remains an asset-value cross-check, not a cap. The frozen 9/19 trade setup keeps its first terminal event and distinguishes target touch from fill.

The preview uses the [TWSE 2026 holiday schedule](https://www.twse.com.tw/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule?response=json&queryYear=2026) for data freshness. For 2027, it fails closed until the next-year calendar is imported. This is intentional; an unknown calendar is not treated as a trading day or a holiday.
