# T06 and T10 native TWSE historical close preparation

The private preparation inspects the registered 11764 date-specific CSV. It validates the raw hash, exact report date, cutoff clock, current official-use policy and selected trading calendar. It joins the frozen active common-stock manifest to the exchange identity known at the historical close before associating a symbol with a stock. An issuer registered later cannot inherit another issuer's closing history. [Official catalog](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11764) · [Prior catalog and rights evidence](../../docs/operations/2026-11-15/launch-radar/TWSE-11764-ACTUAL-FOLLOWUP.md)

The result carries raw close, the observed and preparation clocks, and the source, calendar and roster digests. An explicit missing close remains null. The CSV parser ignores the valuation fields and does not infer open, high, low, shares, turnover or adjusted prices. The absent publisher revision stays null.

The dated November 13 file was retrieved November 15 with 1,086 rows and passed the parser in an isolated PostgreSQL 17.11 cluster. A synthetic one-issuer roster and calendar passed preparation and the negative controls for late catalog knowledge, pre-close observation, bad digest, current rights withdrawal, later instruments and a newer calendar cancellation. The isolated test does not establish application knowledge or ranking on November 13. The new routines are owner-only and write no capture or ranking.
