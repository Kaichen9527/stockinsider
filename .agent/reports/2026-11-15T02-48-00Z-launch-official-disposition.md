# Official disposition and suspension controls

The existing launch qualification has no native positive disposition coverage and preserves unknown. A Contabo read-only comparison checked official open datasets and separate dated reports for both markets.

- TWSE government dataset 29082 supports the dates on its registered CSV route. The available default on November 15 returned only 2455 and 3189. The 11/13 session-specific request returned those and three additional instruments. Ordinary shares 2360 and 3532 both had an announced interval 11/09–11/13 inclusive. A default-list absence would miss actual disqualifications on that session. The 11/06 and 11/13 selectors returned distinct hashed bodies.
- TPEx catalog 11396 returned five current records. The dated report explicitly returned the requested 11/13 interval and those five records plus one blank sentinel. It uses an exact market and full security ID. Convertible bond 24552 being under disposition says nothing about OTC ordinary stock 2455. A separately sourced TWSE disposition also exists for ordinary stock 2455. The 11/06 date report returned eight material rows involving seven distinct securities. Route scope for the dated web report must be checked independently from the registered open CSV.
- TWSE 11677 paired 2314's 11/10 08:00 suspension and 11/11 08:00 resumption. TPEx 48665 exposes separate records for suspension and resumption, for example 1788 on 6/18 08:00 and 6/22 08:00. Preserve the two clocks and identity, attaching the most recent effective market event at the cutoff. On 11/15, TPEx 48664's OpenAPI model reported an empty sentinel explicitly dated 11/14. It cannot clear an earlier unclosed suspension or prove coverage on a different session. An unavailable or undated response is unknown.

Use the actual published interval, point-in-time instrument roster, official exchange calendar and later amendments as distinct inputs. End dates can change in the presence of closures, full-day suspensions or last-trading-day limits; only the event's current confirmed window is usable for absence proof. The amended effective date and when it became known must both be retained. Attention or an intraday opening delay has its own contract.

The timestamped controls were collected after November 13. They prove late overlap semantics and current response shapes. There was no qualifying historical receipt before either cutoff and no writer or runtime switch in this trial.
