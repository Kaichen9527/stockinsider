The launch research implementation needs an independently reviewable preparation mode for candidates with complete mandatory market data and a declared financial gap. It should freeze the real evidence and gap before the author dispatch, remain in the existing execution and attempt ledger, and keep technical-data and source-rights checks intact. Recovered stages need three distinct end-to-end cases: known finance absence with full momentum authority, finance data complete, and technical or rights evidence unavailable. The automatic launch cycle also needs to reach that same queue and writer. Current lookup failures and uncertain write outcomes must retain their existing classifications.
