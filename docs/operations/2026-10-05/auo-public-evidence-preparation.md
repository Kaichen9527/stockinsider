## Implications to test, not settled conclusions

- Separate Micro LED interconnect and glass-core packaging. Shared AI demand does
  not establish the same customer, adoption schedule or profit pool.
- Expand research to the named emitters, detectors, redistribution materials,
  fiber and glass partners. Map each entity to an official instrument separately;
  do not count one AUO release as multiple independent confirmations.
- Require the path from demonstrated engineering to customer qualification,
  orders, capacity/yield/pricing, ownership and depreciation before adding basic
  EPS. The financial effect remains conditional while these inputs are absent.
- Compare alternative architectures and customers' packaging roadmaps, including
  the strongest case for substitution or delay. Technical compatibility alone
  cannot prove an Intel order.

The next research artifact must add primary partner/customer documents, eight
quarters and 24 months of financial evidence, calibrated peer assumptions and
current official market inputs. No invented volume, EPS or high target price is
introduced by this preparation. AUO remains a demonstration research direction.
