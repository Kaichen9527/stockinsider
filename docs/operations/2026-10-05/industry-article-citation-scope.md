# Industry citations in deep research

The original product plan needs industry evidence even when a source never names
an issuer. Company mentions and industry context must remain different evidence.
This amendment changes the shared deep article contract, admission and display;
it does not change ranking, model budget, source ingestion, schema or strategy.

A paragraph may explicitly declare `evidenceScope: industry_context` only in
`industry_position`, with kind `reported` or `inference`, no official-fact IDs,
and at least one source. Every cited document must have stored industry scope.
Other paragraphs, catalysts, orders and scenario calculations retain direct
company-source requirements. Old unioned company tags never convert an industry
source into company evidence. Unchanged legacy paragraphs keep their existing
hash behavior. The scope and references are included in the article hash.

The shared citation policy requires explicitly stored public visibility,
public-citation rights and public document/publisher-transcript acquisition for
industry sources. It accepts an exact public Threads post permalink even when
its author handle contains InvestAnchors; member pages, profiles, spoofed hosts,
private or authenticated necessary-summary acquisition remain excluded. Generic
company/dossier member restrictions are unchanged. Malformed source metadata
fails closed. Admission and source-link display call the same policy.

Every existing cutoff, publication/observation time, substance, revision and
withdrawal barrier remains. Industry paragraphs display their limited scope.
This is structural validation, not a semantic claim-support oracle: the separate
reviewer still checks whether each sentence is actually supported. Nothing in
this amendment establishes AUO orders, eligible investment status or new EPS.
The actual locally read Threads industry item remains bounded authenticated
summary and therefore is not made publishable by this amendment.
