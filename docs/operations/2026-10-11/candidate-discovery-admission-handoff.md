## Admission contract

General admission requires a pair with different canonical publishers/authors AND different independent news roots. Platform count is display information, never a hard gate. Same-platform original authors/roots qualify; one creator across platforms and multiple reposts of one root do not. Root/publisher identities must come from resolved lineage, not platform-prefixed legacy keys, source URLs, or content hashes. Current retracted roots are excluded conservatively.

The separate PTT exception requires one current stock-bearing article and at least twenty distinct reply authors whose actual text and verified mention symbols identify the stock. Bare reactions, unrelated replies, duplicate authors, and pooling replies across articles do not qualify. This implementation checks explicit ticker text as a conservative lower bound; company-name-only replies remain unsupported until a verified mention resolver supplies appropriate evidence. Total push counts are never inputs.

Reasoned new major official events or high-impact leads can occupy up to five low-volume slots, explicitly not multi-source confirmation. The pure projection preserves every input/audit, takes the existing input ranking unchanged, and selects at most fifteen general plus five low-volume candidates. `schedulingIntegrated:false` is intentional. Weekly five-new-deep-research policy is displayed but remains enforced by the existing guarded priority/budget pipeline; this library creates no queue, claims, reservations, or budget ledger.

## Quote and valuation applicability

Candidate-stage price is only positive finite `technical.close` with a valid civil `technical.sessionDate`; `valuation.currentPrice` is never a fallback. An explicit official latest-complete-session argument can distinguish older/current/future sessions. The current card contract lacks that authority, so the UI says latest session needs verification. No seven-day TTL, availableAt, wall-clock weekday heuristic, or hardcoded holiday date declares a current price. The Oct9 holiday/Oct8 latest-session example is tested as supplied authority, not inferred.

PB/TTM values are labelled historical references with method and unknown valuation-source date; stale/unbound targets and upside are hidden. No forward reasonable target or mixed-date upside is claimed. The older source-signal/stock-card fallback branches lack this stage contract and are outside this increment; further caller integration and dated valuation provenance remain required.

## Full mention / calendar adapter inventory

The existing full main mention read in `loadCandidateStageCards` selects `stock_id, platform, source_name, author_name, source_url, stance, independent_content_hash, mentioned_at, available_at, publisher_key, publisher_name, provenance, stocks(...)`, pages 750 rows up to 100,000 with requireComplete before grouping and truncating display links to eight. These columns can support direct company/accounting/clocks and raw identity inspection, but no independent message-root ID or verified cross-platform creator mapping is selected. Source-v2 producer mention provenance copies only retention_mode, source_entity_id, canonical_content_hash, canonical_publisher_key and stance semantics (plus GDELT matcher fields); it omits per-reply evidence. Neither content hashes nor platform-prefixed publisher keys satisfy the new general admission identity.

PTT raw document metadata for `source_surface=ptt_stock_push` contains `author_name, parent_article_author, parent_document_url, comment_index, comment_tag, evidence_excerpt, matched_symbols, matched_symbol_reason` and `retention_mode=metadata_local_excerpt`. Article metadata retains only its first twenty `comment_evidence` excerpts and aggregate push counts, so that array alone cannot establish complete author accounting. The raw body is intentionally not retained (article contentText is title). Main mentions have source_document_id=null and do not retain the reply fields. A future bounded raw-metadata join must prove original article body company mention/current revision, reply-level matching, complete capture and author/root identity; it cannot manufacture them from URLs, truncated evidence or total pushes. This successor does not add such a join or admit fabricated candidates.

`loadOfficialSessions` already uses service-role RPC `candidate_research_official_sessions_page(p_cutoff,p_page_offset,p_page_limit)` with a 1,320-session bound. Its original function in `20260831_candidate_research_official_calendar.sql` uses latest recorded heads, three source/collection/recorded cutoff clocks, close<=cutoff, one semantic head, completed status, and TWSE only. It is reusable for a future saved TWSE latest-complete-session projection with explicit cutoff and evidence; it cannot certify TPEx separately or represent a later wall-clock day when the saved snapshot has an older cutoff. The existing stage public payload has no official latest-session provenance field. `technical.sessionDate` is only a stock's observation date. No latest-session adapter is added to old immutable payloads, no current-price authority is inferred, and the missing authority remains visible.
