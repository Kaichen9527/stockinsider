The key new boundary is enforceability: old acquire/release RPCs do not update an archive ledger; acquired_at and owner equality cannot prove no ABA. Controlled acquisition needs an exact backend-to-live-row binding and an independent, never-reused archive attempt generation. A narrow future observer must invalidate archive bindings on all relevant legacy mutations, or an independently proved equivalent must replace it. This proposal tests an observer only in a disposable schema; it does not authorize a shared production trigger or modify old helpers. Generic same-owner renewal, stale recovery, writer-activation release and cutover remain in the adversarial matrix.

Principal authority is append-derived. Current-row locks miss a newer revoked or conflicting binding, so the proposed fixture tests relation predicate protection alongside the existing lease→writer→backend→settings order. Holding authority locks across file I/O is forbidden. The fixture must establish actual blocking/serialization with separate sessions and pg_locks; no guessed ordering or sleep-only proof.

The next executable result is measured facts, not quota activation: heap/TOAST/index, LSN-generated versus retained WAL, artifact/staging allocated blocks deduplicated by dev/ino, unresolved failed allocations and host/memory margins. Existing128MiB raw allowance does not equal shared4GB physical capacity. Financial/audit writers currently allocate before registering a receipt; their absence from shared reservations remains a real blocker to a production shared-cap claim even if archive tests pass.

After review, root can queue one <=240-second disposable PG17/FD invocation with <=10-second cleanup and the finite fixture limits in spec. Missing metrics/tools, blocked ownership privileges, timeout, child-close uncertainty or failed required checks must retain a negative report. Synthetic identities/stub transports and RLS differences are listed in the report. No real source/model, production authority, permanent root, backup restore or eviction is implied.

Docs-only repair after independent e0b9 P2 findings: all controlled acquire/renew/takeover/verify/terminal fixture entrypoints now reject non-READ COMMITTED before replay or mutation. Actual two-session acceptance must show a pre-established REPEATABLE READ snapshot missing B's committed revocation despite relation locks; the guarded path rejects it, while READ COMMITTED reads the revoke. No such test has been run in this documentation slice.

A second distinct repair covers authority ABA while the lease is unchanged. The proposed isolated fixture freezes exact authority heads and an archive-local immutable-journal fingerprint/count. Committed principal/backend/writer/settings mutations append immutable invalidation events, so active→revoked/off→active cannot reuse old verification. The journal fingerprint grants no authority and does not replace the established registry. Every prior attempt/reservation is retained; a new CAS attempt requires fresh actual readback. Lease-only or partially instrumented fixtures explicitly refuse old verification and report incomplete coverage. A production observer/journal does not exist by virtue of this proposal, and unsupported privileged observer discontinuity cannot be papered over with a fresh active check.
