## Important false shortcuts / missing contracts

1. **evidence_root_id already exists but is not usable for independent-news confirmation.** web/src/lib/opportunity-v3/source.ts:103 claimsFromDocument sets evidenceRootId=canonicalClaimHash. Different paraphrases may yield different hashes for the SAME origin; identical claims may arise from different events. Reuse it for existing dedup only. New discovery policy needs a separately versioned, evidence-backed root binding, never reinterpret old immutable rows.
2. No dedicated canonical creator-link/cross-platform equivalence relation was found in the inspected web/scripts/config/base source contracts. Publisher authority + exact revision publication verification are usable building blocks, but same-author different platforms must be explicitly reconciled to one approved identity. source_entities.platform/source_key, legacy publisher_key, profile URL, roster profile IDs and distribution_identity do not prove that by themselves.
3. Legacy candidate_source_mentions.source_document_id FK is **source_raw_documents(id)** (20260830_source_ranking_v2.sql:73), not source_document_revisions_v3. research-v2.ts upsertSourceRawDocuments:2251 still writes null. Raw document content/entity/url/collection time and normalized v3 revision must be explicitly reconciled under known-at clocks; never cast IDs or URL-join silently. If two candidates match, fail closed. Source/content hashes can verify equality only, not origin independence.
4. Source publication verification proves issuer/publisher provenance, not an independent underlying report, interview, filing or event. A versioned trusted origin binding must say which exact revisions/claims share an origin and which reviewed primary roots differ, with evidence/role/time/revocation receipt. There is no already-authoritative general-purpose news-root resolver exposed by the inspected stage-card path. This is a small missing contract, not grounds for inventing a whole new research pipeline.

Collector samples first20 comments; no assertion of all replies. Conversation URL is a document grouping key, NOT an independent-news root.

A lower-bound20 rule need not pretend all replies were read, but must witness20 distinct current explicit-code replies under ONE current stock-bearing parent. Current article matched_symbols come from title+body combined: they do not prove ticker in original body. Do not manufacture body='2409' from this flag. Local PTT account identity can be used for this one-post exception; it does not need or imply cross-platform canonical creator equivalence.
