# StockInsider Engineering Constitution

## I. Evidence Before Recommendation

Every investment-research result must retain point-in-time provenance, distinguish observation from verified evidence, and fail closed when required evidence is missing or contradictory. Social discussion may discover a candidate but cannot silently become authoritative financial evidence.

## II. Deterministic, Bounded Research

Candidate selection, enrichment, scoring, valuation and evaluation must have explicit workload/egress bounds, stable ordering and versioned inputs. The same canonical inputs must produce the same terminal result; corrections create new immutable lineage.

## III. Research and Action Are Separate

Formal research status, new-position research action and existing-position context are separate typed decisions. The product must not manufacture a buy, infer user holdings or weaken an existing publication gate to improve apparent opportunity counts.

## IV. Point-in-Time Safety

Historical runs and evaluations may use only authority, facts, approvals and source material available at their cutoff. Look-ahead, post-ranking backfill and selection among duplicate daily cohorts are prohibited.

## V. Additive, Guarded Change

New database structures are additive, RLS-protected and written only through authenticated server paths. No Loop change authorizes merge, push, deploy, production migration or model influence unless its later checkpoint states that authority explicitly.

## VI. Executable Acceptance

Approved acceptance inventory is canonical. Every inventory ID must map one-to-one to an executable, non-skipped case; a Gate passes only with zero blocker/high findings and complete reproducible evidence.

## Governance

This constitution is subordinate only to explicit user authority and repository safety instructions. Requirements or designs that conflict with it must be amended before implementation. Architecture approval does not imply implementation or production authority.
