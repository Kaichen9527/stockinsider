# Sector Scoring Reference Contract: source-led-opportunity-engine-v3

Version: `sector-reference-v3.1`

Scoring and sector-cycle inputs use one immutable point-in-time reference manifest at `sourceCutoff`. Constituents are cutoff-valid active common stocks from the bound instrument-roster and sector-taxonomy manifests; `unknown` is excluded from cross-sector percentiles. Every completed-session endpoint comes only from `trading-calendar-contract.md`, its observations bind the applicable exchange authority IDs and the header stores the covering `tradingCalendarWindowHash`. Source mentions, free-form sectors, future constituents and assistive models have no role.

For each canonical sector, freeze constituents sorted by symbol. Price inputs are official corporate-action-adjusted TWSE/TPEx closes for the latest completed session and the aligned 5/20/60-session prior closes. A sector price aggregate requires at least five constituents and observations for at least 80% of its frozen constituents at both endpoints. Constituent return is `adjustedClose_end / adjustedClose_start - 1`; sector return is the arithmetic mean of qualifying constituent returns. Candidate sector-excess return in percentage points is `100 * (candidateReturn - frozenSectorReturn)`. Sector advance/decline breadth 20d is `100 * advancing / (advancing + declining)`, with unchanged constituents excluded from that denominator; zero advancing-plus-declining is missing.

The sector-cycle market benchmark is the equal-weight mean of official adjusted constituent returns across the entire bound active common-stock roster at the same end/prior sessions. It is available separately for 20d and 60d only when at least 500 symbols and at least 80% of the roster have both endpoints. `sector_excess_return_20d = 100 * (sectorReturn20d - marketReturn20d)` and the 60d formula is identical. No TAIEX, seed, candidate or source-mentioned subset may replace this equal-weight benchmark.

### Sector-excess percentile populations

The two score-affecting population keys are exactly `sector_excess_return_5d` then `sector_excess_return_20d`; they are market-wide across the bound active common-stock roster, never sector-local, candidate-only or seed-sampled. For each key and every roster symbol, emit exactly one terminal row. An included row is `[featureKey,symbol,canonicalSector,valuePct,endSession,priorSession,endAdjustedClose,priorAdjustedClose,sectorReturnPct,endSourceRef,priorSourceRef,corporateActionVersion,rosterManifestHash,taxonomyAssignmentManifestHash]`. An excluded row is `[featureKey,symbol,canonicalSectorOrUnknown,reason,endSessionOrNull,priorSessionOrNull,endSourceRefOrNull,priorSourceRefOrNull]`.

Include a symbol exactly when its canonical sector is non-`unknown`, that sector's applicable aggregate passes the five-constituent/80%-endpoint rule, and the candidate has cutoff-visible fresh finite official adjusted closes with positive prior close at both identical sessions. Otherwise select one exclusion reason in this precedence: `provider_conflict`, `future_observation`, `unknown_sector`, `insufficient_sector_constituents`, `insufficient_sector_endpoint_coverage`, `stale_observation`, `missing_candidate_endpoint`, `non_finite_value`, `non_positive_denominator`. No outlier removal, liquidity screen, source screen, sampling or candidate-membership filter occurs.

Rows sort feature-key enum order then symbol ascending. For each key, `rosterCount = includedCount + sum(exclusionReasonCounts)` must equal the bound roster size. A percentile population is available only when `includedCount >= 500` and `includedCount / rosterCount >= 0.80`; otherwise all candidate uses of that key are unavailable even if a candidate has an included row. Available candidate percentiles apply the Type-7 winsor/midrank algorithm in `scoring-contract.md` to every included `valuePct`. Thus identical manifests admit one population and one result.

The price population is bounded to two terminal keys times at most 20,000 roster symbols, or 40,000 terminal rows. Its builder queries only the four required endpoint observations per symbol plus the latest completed session identity under roster-bounded SQL predicates. Observing a 20,001st roster symbol fails `roster_volume_exceeded`; duplicate terminal rows, a conservation error or an unknown reason fail data integrity. No row is loaded and later truncated in application memory.

Financial aggregates use the latest cutoff-valid official/FinMind-mirror facts selected by `financial-data-contract.md`. For each sector/input, require at least five constituents and at least 60% of frozen constituents with a fresh comparable value. Compute the Type-7 median of constituent monthly-revenue YoY, quarterly EPS YoY, latest monthly YoY minus its prior-three-month mean, and quarterly operating-margin YoY delta. Missing/non-finite/stale facts are excluded and counted by typed reason; they never become zero.

Cross-sector percentiles require at least eight non-`unknown` sectors whose applicable aggregate is complete at the identical cutoff. They use Type 7/midrank math from `scoring-contract.md`. A candidate/sector value whose own aggregate is unavailable makes that subfeature unavailable.

### Canonical aggregate rows and manifest

Aggregate-key enum order is exactly: `sector_return_5d`, `sector_return_20d`, `sector_return_60d`, `sector_excess_return_20d`, `sector_excess_return_60d`, `sector_ad_breadth_20d`, `sector_revenue_yoy_median`, `sector_eps_yoy_median`, `sector_revenue_acceleration_median`, `sector_operating_margin_delta_median`. For every key and every non-`unknown` sector present in `sector-taxonomy-map-v3.json`, emit exactly one included aggregate or excluded aggregate.

Before aggregation, let `K` be the number of roster symbols assigned to a non-`unknown` canonical sector at the cutoff, let `U` be the number assigned `unknown`, and let `R=K+U` be the bound active common-stock roster count. A sector key freezes one terminal constituent tuple `E=[symbol,valueOrNull,observedAtOrNull,sourceRefOrNull,terminalCode]` for each symbol assigned to that exact non-`unknown` sector, sorted symbol. `terminalCode` is one of `used`, `provider_conflict`, `future_observation`, `stale_observation`, `missing_observation`, `non_finite_value`, `non_positive_denominator`; authority/freshness resolution selects the first applicable code in that order. `aggregateEvidenceHash = SHA256(UTF8(RFC8785(orderedETuples)))` as lowercase hex. These tuples are stored behind the manifest and cannot be rebuilt from newer facts.

The physical `aggregate_evidence_rows` representation is exactly `[aggregateKey,canonicalSectorOrUnknown,E]`. For every one of the ten aggregate keys it stores one row for each of the `K` non-`unknown` roster symbols under that symbol's one sector. It additionally stores one `unknown` row for each of the `U` symbols only under `sector_return_20d` and `sector_return_60d`; `unknown` is invalid for the other eight keys and never creates a sector aggregate. Rows sort aggregate-key enum order, canonical-sector enum order with `unknown` last, then symbol. Thus each non-`unknown` symbol owns exactly ten rows, each `unknown` symbol exactly two rows, and the exact section count is `10*K + 2*U <= 10*R <= 200000`; row 200,001 or any duplicate/missing/extra identity fails `bound_violation` before root completion.

An included aggregate tuple is `[aggregateKey,canonicalSector,value,asOf,endSessionOrNull,priorSessionOrNull,frozenConstituentCount,usedConstituentCount,coveragePct,aggregateEvidenceHash]`. An excluded aggregate tuple has the same fields with `value=null` and appends one reason selected in this order: `provider_conflict`, `future_observation`, `insufficient_constituents`, `insufficient_endpoint_coverage`, `missing_market_benchmark`, `insufficient_financial_coverage`, `stale_observation`, `missing_observation`, `non_finite_value`, `non_positive_denominator`. Price/excess/breadth rows use the price rules above; financial rows use the five-constituent/60% rule. `coveragePct = roundHalfAwayFromZero(100 * usedConstituentCount / frozenConstituentCount,2)`, null only when the frozen count is zero.

The two market-benchmark terminal tuples, in 20d then 60d order, are `[horizon,valuePctOrNull,endSession,priorSession,rosterCount,usedSymbolCount,coveragePct,aggregateEvidenceHash,reasonOrNull]`; reason uses `provider_conflict`, `future_observation`, `insufficient_endpoint_coverage`, `stale_observation`, `missing_observation`, `non_finite_value`, `non_positive_denominator` precedence. The 20d benchmark evidence sequence is every stored `E` below `aggregateKey=sector_return_20d`, across all 32 sectors plus `unknown`, re-sorted by symbol alone; the 60d sequence is identical under `sector_return_60d`. The benchmark hashes those exact full-roster sequences. A non-`unknown` tuple is therefore byte-reused from its sector-return evidence and is never duplicated; an `unknown` tuple exists only to complete the corresponding benchmark sequence. `rosterCount` equals the sequence count `R`, and `usedSymbolCount` equals its `terminalCode=used` count. Every sector-excess aggregate references the applicable benchmark value through the shared sessions and fails `missing_market_benchmark` when it is null.

Aggregate rows/exclusions sort aggregate-key enum order then canonical sector. Conservation rows are `[aggregateKey,expectedSectorCount,includedCount,excludedCount,exclusionReasonCounts,expectedEvidenceCount,storedEvidenceCount]` in aggregate-key order, where every closed reason key is present including zero and `expectedSectorCount = includedCount + excludedCount`. `expectedEvidenceCount` is `R` for `sector_return_20d|sector_return_60d` and `K` for every other key; `storedEvidenceCount` must equal it. The ten stored counts must sum to the section equation `10*K+2*U`. The static `financialContractHash = SHA256(UTF8("opportunity-financial-inputs-v3.3")) = fc1163fc49f77c643018c542e96b417555be39078b8b3aa6fde98a59a45b2bdc`; a semantics change requires that contract version and hash to change.

Under `manifest-storage-contract.md`, cutoff and the roster/taxonomy/provider/corporate-action/financial dependencies are the `sector_scoring_reference` header. The exact tuples above populate, in order, `market_benchmark_rows`, `sector_aggregate_rows`, `sector_aggregate_exclusions`, `aggregate_evidence_rows`, `sector_excess_included_rows`, `sector_excess_excluded_rows` and `conservation`; every aggregate evidence row carries its aggregate key and canonical-sector-or-unknown identity before `E`, so the section ordering and both reuse projections are total. Completion independently recomputes every sector hash, both full-roster benchmark hashes, the `10*K+2*U` section count and all ten conservation counts from stored rows. A duplicate terminal/conservation/evidence row, invalid `unknown` branch, missing/extra roster identity, count above 200,000, unknown key/reason, non-finite value, differing complete-authority tie or hash mismatch fails before the root can complete. `sectorScoringReferenceManifestHash` is the universal root hash. Logical kind `sector_scoring_reference` in the generic manifest tables stores the immutable pages/root and opaque evidence refs; there is no dedicated `sector_scoring_reference_manifests_v3` physical table. A preparing manifest job freezes it before `seal_inputs`; the market/reference manifest and final logical key bind it, and no live row can reconstruct it after seal.
