The amendment begins from these reproducible observations:

- `recommendations` and `story_candidates` retained the same 44-symbol set from
  2026-07-19 through 2026-07-29 while recommendation jobs rewrote the set 20–31 times
  per day.

  2026-07-29.

  discovery projection reports zero discovered stocks.
- the live Web consumer is the reviewed July deployment, but its local producer is an
  untracked May copy of `.agent/scripts/auth-source-worker.js`; `runtime_doctor.js`
  reports that producer as stale.
- the 2337 bridge combines annual revenue 56.39 billion and operating margin 10% with
  hard-coded EPS 30.04. Even before tax and non-operating adjustments, dividing the
  implied 5.639 billion operating profit by 1.969 billion weighted shares yields only
  about 2.86 per share.
- below-support cards can describe a higher-than-market level as a pullback and can
  display an invalidation/stop above the current long entry.
- existing freshness, valuation and technical audits can pass those defects.
- uncached production homepage and radar reads take tens of seconds and concurrent
  radar reads can exceed 60 seconds.

These observations are RED evidence only. They are not synthetic promotion cohorts and
must never be copied into evaluation-governance maturity.
