# TWSE 11764 automatic acquisition and committed receipts

The existing daily plan adds work for the latest 30 verified TWSE sessions, newest first, after its previous index and daily resources. It checks the active research pool, verified calendar and registered ordinary-share roster. It uses the existing owned source enqueue and respects both the remaining capacity of this plan and the number of admitted bindings for the Taipei date. A matching same-day binding is left in place. A stable v3 plan reports newly admitted close jobs as `twseCloseJobs` when present. The response still carries no source or calendar authority.

The private receipt selector uses the latest resource binding that already existed at the cutoff. An incomplete or incompatible latest binding keeps the report unavailable. A usable receipt verifies the exact artifact, committed outcome, acknowledgement, reporting session, registered roster, current source rights and timestamp bounds. This receipt authorizes subsequent private selection only. Comparable market breadth still requires the separate price, instrument and company-action rules and coverage across both markets.

The licensed worker claims the current-date 11764 report first. A previous generation remains available for audit in the binding ledger. Its job cannot be re-claimed in the licensed queue or adopted into the ordinary acquisition path. Other licensed sources and ordinary claims retain their existing rules.

Native staging checks used an actual dated 11764 CSV and explicitly simulated calendar, roster, policy and lease dependencies. The planner count and cutoff checks are fixture evidence. Production flags, schedules and data were not changed.
