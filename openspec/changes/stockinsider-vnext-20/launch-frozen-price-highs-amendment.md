# Price highs from the frozen chart

The launch detail derives its price-high comparisons from the displayed daily chart of the selected ranking run. The chart reader first validates the run cutoff and binds the price, calendar and corporate-action revisions. The existing chart producer checks the completed market sessions and pins their observations and adjustments. The browser receives the derived values alongside the existing chart revision. No live price, source acquisition or additional RPC is required.

A new high means the signal session's adjusted intraday high strictly exceeds the highest adjusted high among prior sessions. A tie leaves the prior high in place. The comparison excludes the signal session and the 239 indicator warmup rows. The 25-session comparison requires 25 earlier displayed sessions. The 52-week comparison starts 364 calendar days before the Taiwan signal date and requires a chart extending through that boundary. A known-period comparison uses the prior displayed candles and reports the first observed date. All-time coverage remains unknown without a complete from-listing history. These observations describe price history and carry no points or entry authority.
