# Frozen reviewed scenario prices

A public ranked detail can show the original reviewed bear, base and bull prices in TWD per share. The database selects the publication, input hash, qualification and independently settled author and reviewer from the requested immutable rank. It rechecks the original evidence hashes and clocks against the approved claims. It also requires an active deployment-managed disclosure approval keyed by the research revision and exact author output hash, recorded no later than the ranking cutoff. Review or research-use scope alone does not grant public disclosure. A newer thesis never replaces a saved version. Source permissions, withdrawal and grant expiry can suppress a saved projection.

The read routine returns only the method, common period, original quote and valuation dates, and the three ordered prices. With an explicit P/E-input disclosure permission for that same output hash and cutoff, the read also exposes each reviewed positive EPS, its basis and the corresponding positive multiple. A price-only approval keeps those fields absent. Removing the additional permission hides the operands while a valid price approval can still preserve the scenarios. Raw sources, reviewer notes and proprietary provider records stay private. The disclosed prices are research scenarios and can all be below the quote. They supply no ranking points, trade signal or probability. The issuer EPS and P/E reference remain separately sourced. A P/E scenario requires two reviewed same-period operands, positive EPS and a positive matching P/E multiple, and must reproduce the submitted price. The model EPS bridge and falsifier disclosure require their own evidenced fields. Missing or inaccessible scenarios stay unavailable.
