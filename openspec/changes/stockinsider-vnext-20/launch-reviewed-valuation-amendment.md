# Frozen reviewed scenario prices

A public ranked detail can show the original reviewed bear, base and bull prices in TWD per share. The database selects the publication, input hash, qualification and independently settled author and reviewer from the requested immutable rank. It rechecks the original evidence hashes and clocks against the approved claims. It also requires an active deployment-managed disclosure approval keyed by the research revision and exact author output hash, recorded no later than the ranking cutoff. Review or research-use scope alone does not grant public disclosure. A newer thesis never replaces a saved version. Source permissions, withdrawal and grant expiry can suppress a saved projection.

The read routine returns only the method, common period, original quote and valuation dates, and the three ordered prices. With an explicit P/E-input disclosure permission for that same output hash and cutoff, the read also exposes each reviewed positive EPS, its basis and the corresponding positive multiple. A price-only approval keeps those fields absent. Removing the additional permission hides the operands while a valid price approval can still preserve the scenarios. Raw sources, reviewer notes and proprietary provider records stay private. The disclosed prices are research scenarios and can all be below the quote. They supply no ranking points, trade signal or probability. The issuer EPS and P/E reference remain separately sourced. A P/E scenario requires two reviewed same-period operands, positive EPS and a positive matching P/E multiple, and must reproduce the submitted price. The model EPS bridge and falsifier disclosure require their own evidenced fields. Missing or inaccessible scenarios stay unavailable.

## Reviewed thesis falsifier

A separate disclosure scope can show a single source-bound falsifier even when no valuation is publishable. The read uses the same saved rank, preparation, author output hash and independent reviewer receipts. The original must be a completed official extraction for the exact stock and have a currently usable source. The existing original locator validator binds the quote internally. The reviewer must support the specific inference and echo the annotation and source hash.

Only the bounded approved inference, the original observation date, and a public official source URL and publication time enter the browser. The original quote, offsets, document, review notes and other research sections remain private. A late or withdrawn falsifier approval, missing source authority, ambiguous claim, or unsupported inference returns a gap. It carries no score, current validity or entry authority.

## EPS build-up

Reconcile quarterly reported diluted EPS using the reporting-period operating income, net non-operating income, tax expense, non-controlling share, explicit ordinary-share numerator adjustment and weighted diluted shares. A model case supplies those terms for the selected forecast period with individually bound origins. An issuer's operating guidance remains a separate basis and cannot create non-operating, tax or share assumptions. An observed historical value must be explicitly restated as a new model assumption when used in a forecast. Use actual known-at clocks and one basic or diluted basis.

The calculation retains each signed accounting stage and the exact EPS numerator and denominator. Missing inputs keep downstream amounts null, while an explicitly sourced zero is valid. Positive annual or next-four-quarters EPS can use a positive, separately supplied P/E multiple. A quarter remains a quarter. The price uses the original fraction before display rounding. This arithmetic does not grant author/reviewer or public-disclosure permission.

### Private scenario review handoff

The existing earnings replay accepts either one bridge or a versioned three-case input. The three cases share the issuer, cutoff, exact duration and basic or diluted denominator basis. For a next-four-quarters projection the start is the next wholly future quarter at the Taipei cutoff. The model period must not be over. A fiscal quarter can retain EPS arithmetic without producing prices for a P/E scenario set.

One case missing a required input, a nonpositive EPS, a missing multiple or a price below display precision leaves the complete price set unavailable. Bear/base/bull prices must be ordered using the exact fractions before presentation rounding. Case-level amounts, known-at timestamps, source references, content hashes and explicit gaps remain available for private review. A canonical calculation hash identifies the entire packet, including changes to evidence. It confers no provider, author, reviewer or public-disclosure authority and is never sent to a public read route. The fixed MediaTek operating-guidance replay uses the same private handoff when the cases share the same EPS basis. It preserves the underlying issuer guidance separately.
