# Committed TWSE daily closes in supported breadth

The private TWSE 20-session breadth comparison can use source 11764 for a missing official close after the immutable capture and separate readiness acknowledgement. The selected receipt must be available at the original cutoff and still consistent with the source rights and official reporting session. The comparison joins the receipt's historical instrument fact to the registered current stock identifier and ticker. A missing price stays missing. If the existing official quote and the 11764 close both exist and disagree, that observation is unusable. When they agree, both provenances contribute to the input revision.

A new daily roster manifest is independent of the historical capture. A prior capture retains its original roster hash and its actual receipt clocks. The comparison intersects immutable facts with current registered members. Daily planning avoids requesting an already acknowledged date again when the older frozen manifest covers all current TWSE members. An unrepresented member does not inherit a price or an acquisition exemption from the previous manifest.

These inputs still follow the existing exact corporate-action reconciliation. The accepted comparison is limited to the supported feeds and does not establish full capital coverage or an actionable market score. The TPEx comparison is unchanged. The native PostgreSQL staging test used an actual TWSE November 13 CSV and fixture dependencies for the surrounding prices, calendar, registered roster, writer and action terms. It is separate from the required two genuine market sessions.
