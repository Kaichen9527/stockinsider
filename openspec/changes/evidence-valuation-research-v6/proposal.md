# StockInsider v6: Evidence-backed Valuation and Research

## Why

The production research cycle can execute while failing to persist evidence, and the
public product currently has no admitted forward valuations or enriched research
receipts. Database constraints and runtime enums disagree, financial acquisition can
repeat without closing method-specific gaps, and Shadow replay is not immutable.

## What Changes

- Align fact and valuation contracts across TypeScript, PostgreSQL and public readers.
- Add authenticated, receipt-backed MOPS and issuer-IR document ingestion.
- Admit a FinMind mirror only after dataset-level license, schema, unit, point-in-time
  and consistency validation; retain the original provider and upstream attribution.
- Produce method-appropriate forward, normalized-cycle, PB/ROE, EV and turnaround
  valuations from immutable evidence revisions.
- Publish company-specific fact research for every valid discovery and require a
  same-revision receipt before labeling prose `codex_enriched`.
- Keep discovery, research value and action timing as three separate lifecycle stages.
- Retire the global thirty-session Shadow requirement. Preserve historical audit
  records and per-stock reproducibility, data integrity and two-close confirmation.
- Move heavy public aggregation to compact published snapshots and paginated detail.

## Non-Goals

- Do not invent target prices, lower promotion gates or manufacture actionable stocks.
- Do not treat social content, an LLM narrative or provider availability as financial
  authority by itself.
- Do not copy InvestAnchors paid content, restore private crawlers or enable BullTalk
  before written data rights exist.
- Do not backfill qualifying Shadow days or infer that a research reference price was
  an investor's trade.

## Approved amendment: 2026-09-08

The user explicitly superseded the global Shadow requirement. Production research
must not read or write a Shadow manifest, count, or observation. Each run freezes
its own evidence cutoff after acquisition; same-day corrections can enter a new
run without adding another close to the individual-stock confirmation streak.
Repair validation and method-specific acquisition completeness, historical paging
and deep backfill, truthful Threads readiness, and deployment retention. Existing
stage thresholds, official-market safeguards and evidence validation remain binding.
